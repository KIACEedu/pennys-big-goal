# Penny's Big Goal

A kids money game by Kachi Amajor (Kid AI Earners, Young Starlets program).

Kids earn, tithe or give first, save toward a goal, share, tell needs from wants, spend wisely, support friends' businesses, check the quality of their work, and learn online safety. Four levels for ages 5 to 13, plus a grown-ups dashboard.

**Quick demo:** press "⚡ Quick demo" on the title screen, or add `#demo` to the link.
